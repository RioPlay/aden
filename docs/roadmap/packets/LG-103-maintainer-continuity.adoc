= LG-103: Maintainer continuity and ownership

== Outcome

Aden can accept, review, release, and respond to security issues when its original
maintainer is unavailable.

== Current evidence

`MAINTAINERS.md` names one BDFL and security reporting relies on the same contact;
crate ownership and emergency succession are not defined.

== Scope

Maintainer, security, ownership, review, and contribution governance documents.

== Acceptance

* Crates and compatibility surfaces have owners or explicit unowned status.
* Schema, migration, and security changes require independent review.
* Succession, inactivity, emergency authority, and decision appeal are defined.
* Security reporting has a backup responder.

== Non-goals

No foundation, trademark, funding, or multi-company governance structure.

== Failure modes and rollback

Paper ownership without willing people creates false confidence. Unfilled roles
remain visibly vacant rather than naming contributors without consent.

== Completion evidence

Role matrix, ownership map, reviewed emergency procedure, and contributor confirmation.
